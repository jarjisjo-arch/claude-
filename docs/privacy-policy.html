Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Pregna AI ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Pregna AI does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. Specifically:

  • No account required — the App works without registration or login.
  • No personal data stored — search history is kept in memory only and is cleared when you close the App.
  • No analytics — we do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools.

2. Camera & Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to photograph medication packaging for analysis. Photos are sent directly to the Google Gemini AI API for ingredient identification and are not stored by us.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Gemini API — used to identify active ingredients in medication images. Images are processed by Google and are subject to Google's terms of service and privacy policy.

4. Data Safety

  • No personal data is collected or shared with third parties by us.
  • No data is sold or used for advertising.
  • Camera and photo access is used only when you explicitly initiate a scan.
  • The App does not request location, contacts, microphone, or any other sensitive permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

Pregna AI is intended for adults (pregnant women and healthcare-adjacent users). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Pregna AI provides medication safety information for informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist before taking any medication during pregnancy.

Safety ratings are based on the Australian Drug Evaluation Committee (ADEC) classification system — a globally recognised framework for prescribing medicines in pregnancy.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address provided on the Google Play Store listing.
